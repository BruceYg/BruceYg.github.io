## About Me
I am a third-year PhD student in Information Engineering at the University of Cambridge, working in the Machine Intelligence Lab under the supervision of [Prof. Bill Byrne](https://sites.google.com/view/bill-byrne/home). My PhD is fully funded by the Cambridge International Scholarship from Cambridge Trust.

I hold an MPhil in Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence from Cambridge. Before that, I obtained my BS from UC San Diego with a double major in Computer Science and Cognitive Science and a minor in Mathematics.

## Research Interests

I study the safety and alignment of language and multimodal models, with a focus on LLM/VLM post-training. My research interests also include Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, Minimum Bayes Risk (MBR) decoding, and neural machine translation.
